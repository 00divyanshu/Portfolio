## Vision
An elite, conversion-focused developer portfolio for Divyansh Sharma (AI & Full-Stack Engineer | Enterprise LLM & RAG Systems) that solves the dual-positioning challenge: immediately winning high-ticket freelance client contracts (startups and businesses needing secure, production-grade AI platforms) while proving technical depth, architectural rigor, and cyber defense resilience to enterprise recruiters and engineering leads.

## Goals
1. **Commercial Conversion for High-Ticket Freelance Work**: Position 3 concrete enterprise-grade service offerings (Custom Secure RAG, Internal AI Automation/Agents, Full-Stack MVP Prototyping) with direct booking/inquiry workflows.
2. **Deep Technical Rigor & Architectural Proof**: Deep-dive into the flagship Multi-Tenant Enterprise RAG Platform with interactive architecture flow, latency/throughput metrics, and security guarantees.
3. **Defense-First Engineering Narrative**: Showcase an automated 32-test cyber defense suite (magic-byte validation, IP rate limiting, path traversal shields, XSS/SQLi sanitization, tenant namespace isolation).
4. **Production-Grade Next.js / React Architecture**: Deliver a fast, responsive, dark-mode-first aesthetic with zinc/slate neutrals, subtle cyan/emerald accents, monospace metadata chips, and accessibility.

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
- Generic student/entry-level portfolio elements (no generic "Hello World" or "Passionate learner" fluff).
- Complex multi-page blog engine in v1 (case study deep-dives and direct live demonstrations take priority).
- Third-party bloated analytics trackers that compromise performance or security.

## Users & Target Audiences
1. **Startup Founders & Tech Executives**: Seeking high-caliber contractors to build secure AI MVPs and internal knowledge engines without hallucination or security leaks.
2. **Enterprise Recruiters & AI/ML Engineering Leads**: Looking for AI engineers who understand retrieval dynamics, vector systems, evaluation pipelines, and hardened backends.
3. **Prospective Consulting Clients**: Looking for immediate proof of capability, clear service deliverables, and a friction-free booking path.

## Constraints
- **Framework**: Next.js 14+ (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React icons.
- **Styling**: Dark mode by default (zinc-950 background, zinc-800 borders, cyan/emerald glowing accents).
- **Performance**: 95+ Lighthouse score, zero layout shift, sub-second initial contentful paint.
- **Deployment**: Vercel / Cloudflare Pages / Netlify compatible.

- [ ] Complete single-page information architecture covering Hero, Client Solutions, Flagship Deep Dive, Skills Matrix, Trust Vault, and Conversion Footer.
- [ ] Enterprise-grade copy calibrated for business ROI and deep technical credibility.
- [ ] Production-ready, modular React (TypeScript) components for `Hero` and `FlagshipProject` with zero compile errors.
- [ ] Clear Roadmap defined in `.gsd/ROADMAP.md` covering end-to-end delivery.
